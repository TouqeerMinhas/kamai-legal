Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information Kam-AI collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone number, profile photo); booking details (address, the location you pick on the map, job description and photos); chat messages; what you send to the AI assistant, including voice recordings; and a notification token for your device. Professionals also give their CNIC number, CNIC photos, a selfie, their services and service area.

2. How we use it

To run bookings, show nearby professionals, deliver chats and notifications, verify professionals, prevent fraud, give support and improve the app.

3. Location

Location is used only when you allow it or pick a place on the map, to show nearby professionals and the job address. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap.

4. AI assistant

Messages and voice recordings you send to the AI assistant are processed by our AI provider (Google Gemini) to create replies and transcripts. Please do not share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information with the assistant.

5. Who we share with

The other side of a booking sees what is needed to do the job, such as name, job details and address. We use service providers to run the app: Supabase (database and hosting), Google (Gemini AI and Firebase notifications) and OpenStreetMap (maps). We do not sell your data.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Identity documents and payout details are stored encrypted, and only authorised staff can see them, with every view logged.

7. Keeping and deleting data

You can delete your account in Settings. We then delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep for a limited time for legal, safety or dispute reasons.

8. Your choices

You can edit your profile, change the app language, turn notification and location permissions on or off in your phone settings, and delete your account.

9. Children

Kam-AI is not meant for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the new date at the top.

How to delete your data

In the Kam-AI app open Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account and personal data are deleted or anonymised, except records we must keep for a limited time for legal, safety or dispute reasons.

If you can't open the app, email admin.kamai111@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account", and we will delete it.
